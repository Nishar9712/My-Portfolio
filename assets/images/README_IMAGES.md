# 🖼️ How to Add Your Own Real Photos & Screenshots

The website has been redesigned into a clean **Bento Grid** with real browser frames, specially built so you can drop in your **actual headshot photo** and **real project screenshots** without any distortion or AI-looking graphics!

---

## 📸 Image Guide & Recommended Sizes

| File Name | Purpose & Location | Recommended Dimensions & Format | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`avatar.jpg`** | **Your Profile Photo** (Hero Section Headshot Card) | **1:1 square or 4:5 portrait**<br>(e.g. `800 x 800 px` or `600 x 750 px`)<br>Format: `.jpg` or `.png` | Drop your real headshot photo here. If removed or missing, the website automatically displays an elegant monogram avatar (`ND`)! |
| **`project_car.jpg`** | **Project 1 Screenshot** (*Car Price Prediction ML App*) | **16:9 widescreen**<br>(e.g. `1280 x 720 px` or `1920 x 1080 px`)<br>Format: `.jpg` or `.png` | Take a screenshot of your Streamlit app running locally or deployed. |
| **`project_funding.jpg`** | **Project 2 Screenshot** (*Startup Funding Dashboard*) | **16:9 widescreen**<br>(e.g. `1280 x 720 px` or `1920 x 1080 px`)<br>Format: `.jpg` or `.png` | Take a screenshot of your Streamlit dashboard charts and tables. |
| **`project_calendar.svg`** (or `.jpg` / `.png`) | **Project 3 Screenshot** (*Discipline Calendar App*) | **16:9 widescreen**<br>(e.g. `1280 x 720 px` or `1920 x 1080 px`)<br>Format: `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.svg` | Screenshot of your calendar web app prototype. |

---

## ⚡ How to Replace Any Image:
1. Name your new photo/screenshot exactly as listed above (e.g., `avatar.jpg` or `project_car.jpg`).
2. Paste it into this folder: `c:\_Skills\PROJECT+FLASK\Portfolio\assets\images\` (overwriting the placeholder).
3. Refresh your browser at **`http://localhost:8000`** (press `Ctrl + F5` for hard refresh). Your real photo will instantly appear in the sleek frame!
